privacy policy_

effective: 2026-05-22 · last updated: 2026-05-22

chill vibes inc. ("we", "us") runs this website. this page explains the small amount of personal data we touch and what we do with it. plain english only.

what we collect

  • your email, if you choose to give it to us via the signup form on the home page.
  • standard server logs from our host (github pages and the form's storage backend), which may include ip address, user agent, and request times. we don't read these unless we're debugging.
  • nothing else. no analytics, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no advertising trackers, no third-party scripts.

how we use it

  • email: one purpose — to send you a short note when our first product is live. that's it. no newsletter, no funnels, no "10 ways" listicles.
  • server logs: kept by our hosts for their own operational reasons. we don't access or build profiles from them.

who else sees it

  • google. the signup form stores submissions in a private google sheet via google apps script. google's standard privacy terms apply to that storage.
  • github. this site is hosted on github pages. github sees the requests required to serve the page.
  • no one else. we don't sell, rent, share, or trade your data.

how long we keep it

email addresses stay on our list until you ask us to remove them, or until we decide the list has served its purpose and delete it. server logs follow our hosts' retention policies.

your rights

you can ask us to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. email us and we'll do it within a reasonable amount of time. depending on where you live, you may also have additional rights under gdpr, ccpa, or similar laws — same answer: email us.

children

this site isn't aimed at anyone under 13. please don't sign up if you're under 13.

changes

if we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top. if we change it in a way that really matters and you're on our list, we'll email you.
