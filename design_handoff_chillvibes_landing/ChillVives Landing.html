so, what is this?_

chill vibes inc. is a new software company that builds its own SaaS products. we are not an agency. we won't build something for you. we are very politely the wrong vendor for that.

we made this website because we were told that businesses need websites — even ones that haven't shipped anything yet. so here it is. there will be real products here soon. promise.

how we work

a few rules we keep_

01

own what we build

every product is ours. no work-for-hire. it lets us pick problems that take years and not freak out about it.

rule one
02

small on purpose

we plan to stay small. small lets us move quickly, choose carefully, and stay weird.

on purpose
03

ship in public-ish

we'll talk openly about what we're building and what we just deleted. except the stealth ones — those are sworn to secrecy.

in public-ish
04

calm by default

no fake urgency. if something is on fire, we'll deal with it. if it isn't, we won't pretend it is.

calm by default

questions we expect_

why does this website exist if you don't sell anything yet?[ + ]

because apparently you need one. also we got tired of explaining "yes the company is real" in DMs. now we can just send a link.

can you build something for us?[ + ]

no, sorry. we're not an agency. we only build our own products. if you need a studio for hire, drop us a note and we'll happily send you a list of friends who do great work.

what are you actually building?[ + ]

three things. one is for indie devs. one involves ai (sorry). one is still a sticky note. see "in the lab" above for the full extent of what we can publicly say.

are you raising? hiring?[ + ]

not raising. not actively hiring. if you're an excellent human who'd love to work on weird software with us, send a note anyway — we'll keep you in mind.

is the mascot for sale?[ + ]

no. he has a name. it's a secret.

stay in the loop
